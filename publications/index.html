2026

  1. CoRL
    PACER: Process-Aware VLA Policy Learning from Human Corrections for Robotic Disassembly
    Chang Liu, Sibo Tian, Litian Gong, and 4 more authors
    In 10th Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), Austin, TX · Accepted , 2026
    arXiv Code Website

    PACER learns from robot rollouts, human corrections, and clean demonstrations through native supervised fine-tuning. It uses task progress, trace source, and execution outcome to weight valid action chunks in the native supervised action loss, then selects a policy through held-out validation. On a held-out UR5e benchmark covering five motherboard component classes, PACER achieves 87/100 successful approach-and-handoff trials, compared with 54/100 for vanilla post-SFT.

  4. JMSE
    Evaluating Large and Lightweight Vision Models for Irregular Component Segmentation in E-Waste Disassembly
    Xinyao Zhang, Chang Liu, Xiao Liang, and 2 more authors
    Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering, 2026
    arXiv HTML

    A benchmark of SAM2 against a lightweight YOLOv8 network for segmenting irregular electronic components, on a new dataset of 1,456 annotated images. YOLOv8 reaches 98.8% mAP50, showing that large pre-trained vision models still need task-specific optimization for industrial disassembly.

  5. arXiv
    NeurRAFT: Robot Motion Planning via Anchor-Level Flow Matching with Clearance-Aware Preference Tuning
    Sibo Tian, Chang Liu, Minghui Zheng, and 1 more author
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.24026, 2026
    arXiv HTML

    A generative motion planner that flow-matches compact anchor waypoints instead of dense trajectories, then applies direct preference optimization to favor larger obstacle clearance. Transfers zero-shot to a Franka robot under noisy, partially occluded depth.

  6. IROS
    Learning When to See and When to Feel: Adaptive Vision-Torque Fusion for Contact-Aware Manipulation
    Jiuzhou Lei, Chang Liu, Yu She, and 2 more authors

    arXiv HTML

    An adaptive fusion strategy for diffusion policies that admits force/torque signals only during contact phases while using vision throughout, improving success rate by 14% over the strongest baseline on contact-rich tasks.
