Experience

A path through mechanical engineering, industrial automation, and robot learning.

I work at the intersection of learning algorithms and physical systems. My background spans mechanical engineering, hands-on industrial support, and robotics research. Time spent supporting machines in the field continues to shape how I think about reliability, usability, and the gap between an experiment and a working system.

Research and engineering

Texas A&M University

Graduate Research Assistant · January 2024–present

In the Control and Robotics Lab, I study robot learning for contact-rich manipulation with Dr. Minghui Zheng. My work includes PACER, SELF-VLA, and the robotic sorting station in RAISE, alongside teleoperation, data collection, and evaluation infrastructure.

The common thread is making learning useful on real hardware: understanding how a policy fails, collecting the right feedback, and connecting the learned behavior to the rest of the robot system. I also mentor two master’s students in robot learning, experiment design, and technical writing.

HeyGears

Senior Technical Support Engineer · April 2021–November 2023

I supported industrial 3D printing across an East Coast region serving more than 70 clients and 300 printers. My responsibilities included field troubleshooting, new-product testing, customer training, and communicating practical hardware and software issues to R&D.

This experience gave me a close view of what users need from automation: dependable operation, understandable failure modes, and solutions that fit an existing workflow. Those priorities carried with me when I returned to research.

University at Buffalo, SUNY

Graduate Research Assistant · August 2019–February 2021

Working with Dr. Minghui Zheng, I developed vision-based collision prediction for manipulators and explored safety-aware motion planning with reinforcement learning. I also worked on audio-based quadrotor fault detection and maintained experimental systems including UR5e robots, drones, and Vicon motion capture.

Logitech

Mechanical Engineering Intern · May–August 2019

I gathered mechanical engineering requirements and developed a sample database and process documentation for new-product introduction in Suzhou, China.
