I work on vision-language-action models for robotic disassembly: learning from human corrections, recovering from failed attempts, and connecting learned policies to manipulation skills.

PACER

CoRL 2026

  • Paper
  • Project
  • Code

We fine-tune VLA policies on robot rollouts, human corrections and demonstrations. Task progress and execution outcomes determine the training weight of each action chunk.

Experiment details

On a held-out UR5e benchmark across five component classes, PACER achieved 87/100 successful approach-and-handoff trials, compared with 54/100 for vanilla post-SFT. The endpoint is approach and handoff to a fixed downstream skill.

SELF-VLA

Preprint · 2026

We combine VLA approach and recovery policies with an explicit library of disassembly skills. A planner selects the skill, and a corrector handles failed attempts.

RAISE

Resources, Conservation & Recycling · 2026

A system for end-of-life phone disassembly, integrating cutting, sorting and battery removal. I built the UR5e sorting station, including perception, hand-eye calibration and integration with Idaho National Laboratory.
