I'm a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/), advised by [Minghui Zheng](https://zh.engr.tamu.edu/). My research focuses on **robot learning for contact-rich manipulation**, particularly robotic disassembly.

I developed [**PACER**](https://pacer-corl.github.io/) to train vision-language-action (VLA) policies from robot rollouts and human corrections, and [**SELF-VLA**](https://self-vla.github.io/) to coordinate learned approach and recovery policies with disassembly skills. I also built an open-source [teleoperation and data collection platform](https://github.com/ChangChrisLiu/DataCollection) for UR5e robots, along with dataset annotation tools and training-data export pipelines.

For **RAISE**, I built the robotic sorting station for end-of-life phones with Idaho National Laboratory, integrating perception, hand-eye calibration, and automated sorting. My earlier research at the University at Buffalo covered vision-based collision prediction, safety-aware motion planning, and audio-based quadrotor fault detection.

Before my Ph.D., I was a Senior Technical Support Engineer at [HeyGears](https://www.heygears.com/) (2021–2023), also working on site as a **Field Application Engineer**. I troubleshot industrial 3D printer hardware and software at customer sites, tested new products, and brought field requirements back to R&D. I also interned in mechanical engineering at Logitech. I hold an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University at Buffalo and a B.S. in Energy and Power Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.
