Research

Learning from experience, coordinating manipulation skills, and building complete robot systems.

My research asks how robots can adapt to physical variation, recover from failed attempts, and perform useful manipulation with limited task data. I use robotic disassembly as a testbed: products differ, actions have prerequisites, and success often depends on precise contact with small components.

This connects three parts of my work: learning better policies from experience, combining learned behavior with explicit skills, and building the data and hardware systems needed to evaluate them.

Selected projects

PACER CoRL 2026

Learning from robot experience and human corrections

PACER improves VLA policies through native supervised fine-tuning, using robot rollouts, human corrections, and clean demonstrations. Task progress and execution outcomes determine how each valid action chunk contributes to learning.

87/100 successful approach-and-handoff trials, compared with 54/100 for vanilla post-SFT, on a held-out UR5e benchmark across five component classes.

Tools for robot learning

I built and open-sourced a teleoperation and data collection platform based on GELLO. It connects UR5e control over ZMQ, dual-camera capture, phase-labeled recording, and dataset export for OpenVLA, OpenVLA-OFT, and openpi workflows. I also develop annotation tools and a separate dual-joystick teleoperation system.

These tools support the full experimental workflow: collect demonstrations, train policies, run them on the robot, record failures, and collect corrections.

Public dataset releases are available on my Hugging Face profile.

Beyond individual policies

I also collaborate on vision and torque fusion for contact-aware manipulation, generative motion planning, and component segmentation for e-waste disassembly. Our analysis of large language models in remanufacturing examines how these capabilities can fit into a broader automation workflow.

My ongoing interests include multimodal sensing, learning from human feedback, and evaluation across different physical products. See the publication list for published work and preprints.
